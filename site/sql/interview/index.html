    CHECK THE BUSINESS MEANING BEHIND THE NUMBERS

    Reconcile healthcare financial reporting

    This fictional expense-reporting example uses departments, account mappings, reporting periods, budgets, actuals and staged imports. It introduces the reporting concepts a support specialist must clarify with a business owner; it is not a model of a specific vendor’s database.

    Reporting grain

    Know what one row represents. Aggregate ledger transactions to department and period before joining a department-period budget.

    Variance convention

    Here, expense variance = actual − budget. Positive means unfavorable. Revenue reports or an organization’s presentation convention may differ; confirm the definition.

    Period and units

    Amounts are cents. Use the full reporting window and confirm the real system’s timezone and posting rules. Do not mix cents and display currency.

    Reconciliation

    Department subtotals can look correct while unmapped ledger rows are missing. Report exceptions separately and account for them before claiming completeness.

    Discussion guide

    In the base data, Clinical Services has 200000 cents of budget and 218000 cents of actual expense: 18000 cents, or 9%, unfavorable. Support Services is 20000 cents favorable; Research has a budget and no actuals. Department 99 is unmapped and must be reconciled separately. Account codes 6999 and inactive-only 6300 also need review. Additional test data includes a zero budget and boundary timestamps.

    Confirm accounting period, expense/revenue sign convention, applicable mappings, and whether the customer expects posted, imported, or approved activity. Do not call an unexplained difference a product defect without evidence.
