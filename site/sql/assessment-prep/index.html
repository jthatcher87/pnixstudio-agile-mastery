Before you start

Allow 90 uninterrupted minutes: approximately 20 for design, 45 for queries, 15 for troubleshooting and 10 for explanation. This is our practice allocation. The timer continues across refreshes and submits the saved draft when time expires.

Work without AI, hints or reference answers. Run your own SQL and inspect its output; correctness feedback is withheld until submission. Record syntax assistance or other help honestly.
